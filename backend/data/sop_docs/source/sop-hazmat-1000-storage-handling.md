# SOP-HAZMAT-1000: Hazardous Materials Storage and Handling

**Document owner:** Environmental Health and Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-02-15
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs storage, segregation, and handling of hazardous chemicals classified under the facility's GHS (Globally Harmonized System) inventory.

## Section 2. Segregation

Clause 2.1 — Incompatible chemical classes (e.g., oxidizers and flammables, acids and bases) shall be stored with physical separation of at least 20 feet or a fire-rated barrier, per the facility's compatibility chart.

Clause 2.2 — Flammable liquid storage in excess of 25 gallons outside an approved flammable storage cabinet or room is prohibited.

## Section 3. Container and Labeling

Clause 3.1 — Every container of hazardous material shall bear a GHS-compliant label; an unlabeled or illegibly labeled container shall be quarantined and relabeled before further use.

Clause 3.2 — Secondary containers (decanted from bulk) require at minimum the product name and primary hazard pictogram unless used entirely within the immediate work shift by the person who decanted it.

## Section 4. Spill Response

Clause 4.1 — Spill kits appropriate to the stored material class shall be located within 50 feet of any hazardous material storage area.

Clause 4.3 — A spill exceeding 1 gallon, or any spill reaching a floor drain, requires immediate notification to the Environmental Health and Safety Department regardless of whether it was fully contained.

## Section 5. SDS Availability

Clause 5.1 — A current Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for every stored chemical shall be accessible at the point of use, either physically or via the facility's electronic SDS system.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Chemical inventory and SDS records are filed to the Environmental Health and Safety File and reviewed annually for currency.
