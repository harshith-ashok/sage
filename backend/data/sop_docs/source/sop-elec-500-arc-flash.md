# SOP-ELEC-500: Electrical Safe Work Practices and Arc Flash Protection

**Document owner:** Electrical Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-02-01
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Establishes safe work practices for work on or near energized electrical equipment above 50 volts, including arc flash risk assessment and PPE selection.

## Section 2. Energized Work Permit

Clause 2.1 — Work on energized electrical equipment is prohibited unless de-energization is infeasible (e.g., would create a greater hazard, or is technically impossible for diagnostic testing) and an Energized Electrical Work Permit is approved by the Electrical Safety Department.

Clause 2.2 — Every energized work permit requires a documented arc flash risk assessment specific to the equipment and task, not a generic facility-wide value.

## Section 3. Arc Flash PPE Categories

Clause 3.1 — PPE category is determined from the equipment's arc flash label (incident energy in cal/cm²) per the facility's arc flash study, not estimated in the field.

Clause 3.4 — Equipment without a current arc flash label (study older than 5 years, or following any modification to upstream protective device settings) shall be treated as PPE Category 4 (maximum) until a new study is completed.

## Section 4. Boundaries

Clause 4.2 — The arc flash boundary and limited/restricted approach boundaries shall be physically marked or barricaded before work begins; unqualified personnel are prohibited from crossing the limited approach boundary.

## Section 5. Training and Qualification

Clause 5.1 — Only personnel currently qualified per the facility's Electrical Safety Training program may perform energized work or approach within the restricted boundary. Qualification lapses after 36 months without renewal training.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Energized work permits and arc flash studies are filed to the Electrical Safety File. Retain permits for 5 years; retain the current arc flash study for the life of the electrical system it covers plus the prior superseded version.
