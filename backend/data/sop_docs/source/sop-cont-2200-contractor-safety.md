# SOP-CONT-2200: Contractor Safety Management

**Document owner:** Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-04-01
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs prequalification, orientation, and oversight of contractors performing work on site.

## Section 2. Prequalification

Clause 2.1 — A contractor shall be prequalified (safety statistics review, insurance verification, prior-incident history) before being awarded any work involving hot work, confined space, working at height, or lockout/tagout.

Clause 2.2 — A contractor with a Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR) exceeding twice the industry average for their trade is subject to enhanced oversight (a dedicated site safety escort) for the duration of the contract, or is disqualified at the discretion of the Safety Department.

## Section 3. Site Orientation

Clause 3.1 — Every contractor employee shall complete site safety orientation before beginning work, covering emergency procedures, permit systems applicable to their scope, and site-specific hazards.

## Section 4. Permit Compliance

Clause 4.1 — Contractors are subject to the same permit-to-work requirements (hot work, confined space, LOTO, excavation) as facility employees; a facility-issued escort or Person in Charge shall co-sign any permit for high-hazard contractor work.

## Section 5. Incident Reporting

Clause 5.1 — A contractor shall report any incident, near-miss, or injury involving their personnel to the facility Safety Department within 1 hour of occurrence, regardless of severity.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Prequalification files, orientation completion records, and contractor incident reports are filed to the Contractor Safety File and retained for the duration of the contract plus 5 years.
