# SOP-PSM-2500: Process Hazard Analysis

**Document owner:** Process Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-06-01
**Review cycle:** 60 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs conduct and revalidation of Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) for covered processes under the site's Process Safety Management program.

## Section 2. Revalidation Interval

Clause 2.1 — Each covered process's PHA shall be revalidated at least every 5 years using a recognized methodology (HAZOP, What-If, or equivalent).

Clause 2.2 — A significant process change under SOP-MOC-2400 that was not addressed by the current PHA triggers an interim PHA update for the affected node(s) rather than waiting for the next full revalidation.

## Section 3. Team Composition

Clause 3.1 — The PHA team shall include an operator or supervisor with process-specific experience, a process engineer, and a facilitator trained in the chosen methodology; a PHA conducted entirely by engineering staff without operations input is not acceptable.

## Section 4. Recommendation Tracking

Clause 4.1 — Every PHA recommendation is assigned an owner and resolution date; recommendations shall not be closed as "no action needed" without documented engineering justification reviewed by the Process Safety Department.

Clause 4.3 — A recommendation addressing a safeguard gap for a scenario with potential for serious injury or major loss shall be resolved within 12 months of PHA completion, or an interim safeguard implemented immediately if resolution will take longer.

## Section 5. Records

Clause 5.1 — PHA reports, revalidations, and recommendation tracking are filed to the Process Safety File and retained for the life of the process plus the required regulatory retention period.
