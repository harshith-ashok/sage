# SOP-ABATE-4900: Asbestos and Lead-Containing Material Management

**Document owner:** Environmental Health and Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-02-18
**Review cycle:** 36 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs identification, work control, and abatement of asbestos-containing material (ACM) and lead-containing paint/coatings in facility structures and legacy equipment insulation.

## Section 2. Presumption and Survey

Clause 2.1 — Insulation, gaskets, and coatings installed before 1990 are presumed ACM or lead-containing until sampling by a qualified inspector proves otherwise; work shall not proceed on presumed material as if it were confirmed clean.

## Section 3. Work Control

Clause 3.1 — Any work disturbing confirmed or presumed ACM (drilling, cutting, removal) requires a permit from the Environmental Health and Safety Department specifying the work method (wet methods, containment, negative air) appropriate to the disturbance scale.

Clause 3.2 — Only personnel trained and, where required by scale of disturbance, licensed for abatement work may disturb ACM; general maintenance personnel encountering unexpected suspect material shall stop work and notify Environmental Health and Safety rather than proceeding.

## Section 4. Air Monitoring

Clause 4.1 — Abatement work above the minor-disturbance threshold requires personal and area air monitoring for fiber count (asbestos) or lead concentration, with results reviewed before the area is released for re-occupancy.

## Section 5. Waste Disposal

Clause 5.1 — ACM and lead-containing waste is double-bagged/sealed, labeled, and disposed of per SOP-WASTE-3900 and applicable regulatory manifesting requirements for the specific material.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Surveys, permits, air monitoring results, and disposal manifests are filed to the Environmental Health and Safety File and retained for 30 years per exposure recordkeeping requirements.
