# SOP-PPE-1100: Personal Protective Equipment Selection and Use

**Document owner:** Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-01-01
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Establishes minimum PPE requirements by work area and task, and the hazard assessment process for selecting task-specific PPE beyond the area baseline.

## Section 2. Baseline PPE

Clause 2.1 — Safety glasses, hard hat, and safety footwear (ASTM F2413 rated) are mandatory in all designated process/operations areas without exception, including for visitors and contractors.

## Section 3. Task-Specific Hazard Assessment

Clause 3.1 — A written PPE hazard assessment is required for any task not covered by the area baseline, identifying the specific hazard (chemical splash, impact, thermal, electrical) and the PPE that mitigates it.

Clause 3.3 — Chemical-resistant PPE selection shall reference the specific chemical's SDS permeation/degradation data, not a generic "chemical resistant" label — a glove rated for one chemical class may fail rapidly against another.

## Section 4. Respiratory Protection Interface

Clause 4.1 — Any task requiring respiratory protection follows SOP-RESP-1500 in addition to this procedure; this procedure does not independently authorize respirator use.

## Section 5. Inspection and Replacement

Clause 5.1 — PPE shall be inspected before each use; damaged or degraded PPE (cracked lenses, torn gloves, compromised footwear soles) shall be replaced immediately, not used until end of shift.

## Section 6. Training

Clause 6.1 — Personnel shall be trained on the specific PPE hazard assessment for their task before performing it, including proper donning/doffing and limitations of the assigned PPE.

## Section 7. Records

Clause 7.1 — PPE hazard assessments are filed to the Safety Department File and reviewed whenever the task or the chemicals/equipment involved change.
