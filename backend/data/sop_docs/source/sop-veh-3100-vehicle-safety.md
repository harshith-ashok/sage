# SOP-VEH-3100: Site Vehicle and Driving Safety

**Document owner:** Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-04-10
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs operation of company vehicles and personal vehicles used for company business on and off site.

## Section 2. License and Authorization

Clause 2.1 — Only personnel holding a valid license appropriate to the vehicle class, verified annually against the state motor vehicle record, may operate a company vehicle.

Clause 2.2 — A driver accumulating 2 or more at-fault incidents, or a license suspension, within a rolling 12-month period loses company driving authorization pending review.

## Section 3. Site Speed Limits

Clause 3.1 — Site speed limit is 15 mph in process/operations areas and 25 mph on perimeter roads unless posted otherwise; speed limits are not discretionary based on traffic conditions.

## Section 4. Seatbelt and Mobile Device Policy

Clause 4.1 — Seatbelt use is mandatory for all occupants of any vehicle on site, including passengers in the bed or open cargo area, which is itself prohibited for personnel transport.

Clause 4.2 — Handheld mobile device use (calls, texting) while driving on site is prohibited; hands-free calling is permitted only when road/traffic conditions allow full attention.

## Section 5. Pre-Trip Inspection

Clause 5.1 — A documented pre-trip inspection is required daily for vehicles used in regular site operations (lights, brakes, tires, mirrors); a deficiency affecting safe operation removes the vehicle from service until repaired.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Driving authorization records and incident reports are filed to the Safety Department File and retained for 5 years.
