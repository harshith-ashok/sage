# SOP-QC-2700: Quality Control Non-Conformance Management

**Document owner:** Quality Assurance Department
**Effective date:** 2024-03-20
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs identification, disposition, and closure of non-conforming material, work, or documentation.

## Section 2. Identification and Hold

Clause 2.1 — Any material, weld, or work found to not meet its specification shall be tagged non-conforming and physically segregated or clearly marked to prevent inadvertent use, immediately upon discovery.

## Section 3. Disposition

Clause 3.1 — Disposition (use-as-is, rework, repair, scrap) requires engineering evaluation and sign-off; a non-conformance shall never be dispositioned "use-as-is" solely by the originating department without independent engineering review.

Clause 3.3 — "Use-as-is" disposition on a safety-critical item requires documented engineering justification referencing the specific code/standard margin being relied upon, reviewed by a second qualified engineer.

## Section 4. Root Cause and Trending

Clause 4.1 — Non-conformances are logged and trended quarterly; a repeat non-conformance of the same type from the same source (vendor, work crew, procedure) triggers a root-cause review, not just repeated individual dispositions.

## Section 5. Records

Clause 5.1 — Non-conformance reports and dispositions are filed to the Quality Assurance File and retained for the life of the affected equipment.
