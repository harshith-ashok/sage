# SOP-FIREP-3000: Portable Fire Extinguisher Inspection and Maintenance

**Document owner:** Fire Protection Department
**Effective date:** 2024-01-05
**Review cycle:** 12 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs inspection, maintenance, and hydrostatic testing of portable fire extinguishers.

## Section 2. Monthly Visual Inspection

Clause 2.1 — Every extinguisher is visually inspected monthly: gauge in the operable range, pin and tamper seal intact, no visible damage or corrosion, and unobstructed access. Inspection is logged on the extinguisher's tag with initials and date.

## Section 3. Annual Maintenance

Clause 3.1 — A full annual maintenance inspection by a qualified technician is required in addition to monthly visual checks, including internal examination per the extinguisher type's maintenance schedule.

## Section 4. Hydrostatic Testing

Clause 4.1 — Hydrostatic testing interval is per the extinguisher type: 5 years for most dry-chemical and water types, 12 years for dry-chemical stored-pressure types with a stainless shell, per the manufacturer's specification. An extinguisher past its hydrostatic test due date shall be removed from service immediately.

## Section 5. Out-of-Service Handling

Clause 5.1 — An extinguisher removed from service (discharge, damage, failed inspection) shall be immediately replaced with a fully charged unit of at least equivalent rating at that location — a fire protection gap is not left pending replacement scheduling.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Monthly inspection tags, annual maintenance records, and hydrostatic test certificates are filed to the Fire Protection File and retained for the life of the extinguisher.
