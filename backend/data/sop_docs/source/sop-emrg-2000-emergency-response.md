# SOP-EMRG-2000: Site Emergency Response and Evacuation

**Document owner:** Emergency Management Department
**Effective date:** 2024-01-01
**Review cycle:** 12 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs site response to fire, chemical release, medical emergency, and severe weather, including evacuation and accountability.

## Section 2. Alarm and Evacuation

Clause 2.1 — Upon activation of the site evacuation alarm, all personnel shall proceed immediately to their designated assembly area by the nearest safe route; use of elevators during a fire alarm is prohibited.

Clause 2.2 — Area wardens shall conduct a sweep of their assigned area before evacuating themselves, to the extent it can be done without personal risk.

## Section 3. Accountability

Clause 3.1 — Each assembly area leader shall complete a headcount against the shift roster within 10 minutes of arrival and report status (all accounted for / missing personnel identified) to the Incident Commander.

Clause 3.2 — A report of missing personnel is escalated to the Incident Commander immediately for rescue coordination; personnel shall not re-enter the facility to search for a missing coworker under any circumstance.

## Section 4. Incident Command

Clause 4.1 — The first qualified responder on scene assumes Incident Commander duties until relieved by a more senior qualified responder or the Emergency Management Department; command transfer shall be explicitly acknowledged by both parties.

## Section 5. Shelter-in-Place

Clause 5.1 — For an external chemical release or severe weather event, the Emergency Management Department may direct shelter-in-place instead of evacuation; shelter-in-place areas and HVAC shutdown procedures are posted at each building entrance.

## Section 6. Drills and Records

Clause 6.1 — Full-site evacuation drills are conducted at least annually; drill performance (evacuation time, accountability completion time) is documented and filed to the Emergency Management File, retained for 5 years.
