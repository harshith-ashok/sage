# SOP-WASTE-3900: Hazardous Waste Management

**Document owner:** Environmental Compliance Department
**Effective date:** 2024-05-25
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs generation, accumulation, labeling, and disposal of hazardous waste.

## Section 2. Accumulation Area Requirements

Clause 2.1 — Satellite accumulation points are limited to 55 gallons of hazardous waste (1 quart for acutely hazardous waste) at or near the point of generation; once exceeded, the container is dated and moved to the main accumulation area within 3 days.

Clause 2.2 — The main accumulation area is subject to a 90-day maximum accumulation time from the date on the oldest container; waste not shipped off-site within 90 days is a compliance violation requiring immediate Environmental Compliance Department escalation.

## Section 3. Container and Labeling

Clause 3.1 — Every hazardous waste container shall be labeled with the words "Hazardous Waste," the accumulation start date, and a description of contents; an unlabeled or undated container found in an accumulation area is treated as a compliance finding.

Clause 3.2 — Containers shall be kept closed except when actively adding or removing waste; incompatible wastes shall not be combined in the same container.

## Section 4. Weekly Inspection

Clause 4.1 — Main accumulation areas are inspected weekly for container integrity, leaks, and labeling compliance; findings are logged even when no corrective action was needed.

## Section 5. Manifesting

Clause 5.1 — Off-site shipment requires a completed hazardous waste manifest signed by an authorized representative; the generator copy is retained upon the transporter's departure and matched against the signed return copy from the disposal facility.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Manifests, inspection logs, and training records are filed to the Environmental Compliance File and retained for 3 years.
