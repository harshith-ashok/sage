# SOP-ENV-1400: Environmental Spill Prevention and Response

**Document owner:** Environmental Compliance Department
**Effective date:** 2024-04-15
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs spill prevention control measures (SPCC) and response procedures for petroleum products and regulated chemicals with potential to reach navigable waters or soil.

## Section 2. Secondary Containment

Clause 2.1 — Bulk storage tanks over 660 gallons (petroleum) require secondary containment sized for 110% of the largest tank's capacity, inspected monthly for integrity and accumulated water/product.

## Section 3. Spill Classification and Notification

Clause 3.1 — A spill reaching secondary containment only, fully contained, is a Tier 1 event requiring internal documentation but no external notification.

Clause 3.2 — A spill reaching soil, a storm drain, or any water body is a Tier 2 event requiring immediate notification to the Environmental Compliance Department and activation of the spill response team, regardless of volume.

Clause 3.4 — A Tier 2 spill exceeding the regulatory reportable quantity for the specific substance requires notification to the appropriate environmental agency within the regulatory timeframe (typically 24 hours) — the Environmental Compliance Department, not the field responder, makes this determination and files the notification.

## Section 4. Response Actions

Clause 4.1 — Stop the source if it can be done safely, then contain before attempting cleanup; absorbent material shall never be applied before the source is stopped or isolated.

## Section 5. SPCC Plan Review

Clause 5.1 — The site SPCC Plan is reviewed and re-certified by a professional engineer every 5 years, or immediately following any facility modification that changes storage capacity or containment design.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Spill incident reports and SPCC inspections are filed to the Environmental Compliance File and retained for 5 years.
