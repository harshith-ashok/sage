# SOP-RESP-1900: Respiratory Protection Program

**Document owner:** Occupational Health Department
**Effective date:** 2024-03-05
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs selection, fit testing, and medical clearance for respiratory protection where engineering controls cannot reduce airborne hazard exposure below the applicable exposure limit.

## Section 2. Medical Clearance

Clause 2.1 — Medical clearance from Occupational Health is required before initial respirator fit testing, and re-evaluated every 12 months or immediately if the employee reports a relevant health change.

## Section 3. Fit Testing

Clause 3.1 — A tight-fitting respirator requires a passing quantitative or qualitative fit test on the specific make/model/size before use, repeated annually and whenever facial structure changes (significant weight change, dental work, facial scarring) or a different model is assigned.

Clause 3.2 — Facial hair intersecting the respirator sealing surface invalidates the fit test seal; an employee with such facial hair shall not use a tight-fitting respirator until it is removed.

## Section 4. Selection

Clause 4.1 — Respirator selection (air-purifying vs. supplied-air, cartridge type) is based on the specific contaminant and its concentration relative to the cartridge's assigned protection factor — selection by a qualified person referencing the SDS and exposure monitoring data, not by the wearer's preference.

Clause 4.3 — An atmosphere that is IDLH (immediately dangerous to life or health), or of unknown concentration, requires supplied-air or SCBA; an air-purifying respirator is prohibited in either case.

## Section 5. Cartridge Change-Out

Clause 5.1 — Cartridge change-out schedule is established from the contaminant's breakthrough data and use pattern, documented and posted at the point of use — not left to the wearer's judgment of when breakthrough odor is detected.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Medical clearances and fit test records are filed to the Occupational Health File and retained for the duration of employment plus 30 years.
