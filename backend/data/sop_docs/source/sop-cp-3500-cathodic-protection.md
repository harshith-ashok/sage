# SOP-CP-3500: Cathodic Protection System Monitoring

**Document owner:** Corrosion Engineering Department
**Effective date:** 2024-04-20
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs monitoring and maintenance of impressed-current and sacrificial-anode cathodic protection (CP) systems on buried pipelines and tank bottoms.

## Section 2. Survey Frequency

Clause 2.1 — Pipe-to-soil potential surveys are conducted at least annually at each test station, and rectifier output is checked at least every 2 months for impressed-current systems.

## Section 3. Protection Criteria

Clause 3.1 — A protected structure shall read at least -850 mV (copper-sulfate reference electrode, instant-off) at every test point; a reading less negative than this threshold indicates inadequate protection requiring corrective action.

Clause 3.3 — A test point failing the protection criterion for two consecutive survey periods triggers an engineering evaluation of that structure's corrosion risk and CP system adequacy, not just continued monitoring.

## Section 4. Rectifier and Anode Maintenance

Clause 4.1 — A rectifier found with zero or abnormal output at a bi-monthly check is repaired within 15 days; the affected structure's protection status during the outage is evaluated by Corrosion Engineering.

## Section 5. Records

Clause 5.1 — Survey data and rectifier logs are filed to the Corrosion Engineering File and retained for the operating life of the protected structure.
