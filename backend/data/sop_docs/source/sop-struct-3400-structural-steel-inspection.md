# SOP-STRUCT-3400: Structural Steel and Foundation Inspection

**Document owner:** Civil/Structural Department
**Effective date:** 2024-06-10
**Review cycle:** 60 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs periodic inspection of load-bearing structural steel, pipe racks, and equipment foundations for corrosion, deformation, and settlement.

## Section 2. Inspection Interval

Clause 2.1 — General structural inspection is conducted every 5 years; structures in a corrosive or coastal environment are inspected every 3 years.

Clause 2.2 — A structure exposed to an abnormal load event (vehicle impact, seismic event exceeding design basis, fire) requires inspection within 30 days of the event, regardless of the routine schedule.

## Section 3. Findings Requiring Engineering Evaluation

Clause 3.1 — Section loss (corrosion) exceeding 10% of a member's original cross-section, visible weld cracking, or foundation settlement exceeding 1/2 inch differential requires engineering evaluation before the structure is certified fit for continued service.

## Section 4. Temporary Support

Clause 4.1 — A structural finding presenting immediate collapse risk requires immediate area exclusion and temporary shoring/support before any further engineering evaluation proceeds; the area shall not remain in normal use pending evaluation.

## Section 5. Records

Clause 5.1 — Inspection reports and engineering evaluations are filed to the Civil/Structural Integrity File and retained for the operating life of the structure.
