# SOP-MOC-2400: Management of Change

**Document owner:** Process Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-05-15
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs the review and authorization process for any change to process technology, equipment, procedures, or organization that could affect safety, environmental, or process integrity.

## Section 2. Trigger for MOC

Clause 2.1 — A Management of Change (MOC) review is required for any permanent or temporary change outside established operating limits or equipment specifications, including substitution of equipment with a different specification, even if apparently equivalent.

Clause 2.2 — Replacement-in-kind (identical specification, same manufacturer or an approved equivalent already on the approved vendor list) does not require MOC; any deviation from replacement-in-kind does.

## Section 3. Review Requirements

Clause 3.1 — Every MOC requires review of: process safety information affected, operating procedure updates needed, training needs, and pre-startup safety review requirements before the change is implemented.

Clause 3.3 — A change affecting a safety-critical device (relief valve setting, interlock setpoint, alarm setpoint) requires process safety engineering sign-off, not just the requesting department's approval.

## Section 4. Temporary Changes

Clause 4.1 — A temporary change (e.g., a jumper around an interlock for troubleshooting) requires the same MOC review as a permanent change, plus an explicit expiration date; a temporary change not reverted or converted to a permanent MOC by its expiration date is treated as an unauthorized change.

## Section 5. Pre-Startup Safety Review

Clause 5.1 — Before startup following any MOC affecting a covered process, a pre-startup safety review confirms construction/installation matches the design, procedures are updated, and training is complete.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — MOC forms and pre-startup safety review checklists are filed to the Process Safety File and retained for the life of the affected equipment/process.
