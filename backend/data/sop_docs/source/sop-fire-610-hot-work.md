# SOP-FIRE-610: Hot Work Permit Procedure

**Document owner:** Fire Protection Department
**Effective date:** 2024-01-15
**Review cycle:** 12 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs any work producing open flame, sparks, or sufficient heat to ignite flammable material — welding, cutting, grinding, brazing, and soldering — outside a designated permanent hot work area.

## Section 2. Permit Requirement

Clause 2.1 — A Hot Work Permit is required before starting any hot work outside a designated welding shop, valid for one shift only and specific to one location.

Clause 2.2 — A fire watch is mandatory for the duration of the work plus 30 minutes after work stops, and shall have no other assigned duties during that period.

## Section 3. Area Preparation

Clause 3.1 — Combustible material within 35 feet of the work shall be removed or covered with a fire-resistant blanket before the permit is signed.

Clause 3.3 — Where hot work is performed on a vessel, pipe, or tank that has contained flammable material, atmospheric testing per SOP-CSE-200 Section 2 is required before the permit is issued, regardless of whether confined space entry is also involved.

## Section 4. Fire Watch Equipment

Clause 4.1 — The fire watch shall have a charged fire extinguisher rated for the specific combustible hazard present, verified inspected within the current month per SOP-FIRE-640.

## Section 5. Permit Closure

Clause 5.2 — The permit issuer or fire watch shall inspect the area 30 minutes after work stops and again at the end of shift before signing the permit as closed.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Closed hot work permits are filed to the Fire Protection File and retained for 3 years.
