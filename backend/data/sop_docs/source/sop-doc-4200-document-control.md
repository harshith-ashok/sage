# SOP-DOC-4200: Document Control and Records Retention

**Document owner:** Quality Assurance Department
**Effective date:** 2024-02-14
**Review cycle:** 36 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs creation, revision control, distribution, and retention of controlled site procedures and technical records.

## Section 2. Revision Control

Clause 2.1 — Every controlled document bears a revision number, effective date, and owning department; a superseded revision shall be removed from active use points within 5 business days of the new revision's effective date.

Clause 2.2 — A working copy printed or downloaded from the controlled system is valid only at the moment of printing — users are responsible for verifying they hold the current revision before relying on a printed copy for safety-critical work.

## Section 3. Change Approval

Clause 3.1 — A document revision requires review and approval by the owning department and any department whose work the document governs; a procedure affecting a permit-to-work system requires Safety Department co-approval regardless of which department owns it.

## Section 4. Distribution

Clause 4.1 — Safety-critical procedures (permit systems, LOTO, emergency response) require active distribution/acknowledgment tracking, not passive availability alone — affected personnel must be notified of a revision, not expected to check for updates independently.

## Section 5. Retention

Clause 5.1 — Retention period for each document type is set by its owning department per the applicable regulatory or contractual requirement; the Document Control File itself retains a full revision history for every controlled document for the life of the facility.
