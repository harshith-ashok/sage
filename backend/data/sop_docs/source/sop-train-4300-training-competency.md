# SOP-TRAIN-4300: Training and Competency Verification

**Document owner:** Human Resources / Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-01-08
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs required training, competency verification, and refresher scheduling for personnel performing safety-critical or specialized tasks.

## Section 2. Initial Qualification

Clause 2.1 — Competency for a safety-critical task (LOTO authorization, confined space entry, crane operation, energized electrical work) requires both classroom/knowledge assessment and a demonstrated, observed practical evaluation before independent authorization — knowledge assessment alone is not sufficient qualification.

## Section 3. Refresher Intervals

Clause 3.1 — Refresher training intervals are set per task per the governing procedure (e.g., 36 months for electrical safety qualification per SOP-ELEC-500); where no specific interval is set elsewhere, the default refresher interval is 24 months.

Clause 3.2 — An employee involved in an incident related to a specific competency (e.g., an at-fault forklift incident) requires re-evaluation of that specific competency before resuming the task, independent of their refresher due date.

## Section 4. Training Records

Clause 4.1 — Training records specify the exact procedure revision trained to; a training record referencing a superseded procedure revision does not satisfy current qualification once a safety-critical procedure has been revised — affected personnel require delta training on the changes.

## Section 5. Records

Clause 5.1 — Training and competency records are filed to the Human Resources Training File and retained for the duration of employment plus 5 years.
