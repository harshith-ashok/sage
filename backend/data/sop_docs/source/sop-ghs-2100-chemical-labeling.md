# SOP-GHS-2100: Chemical Labeling and Safety Data Sheet Management

**Document owner:** Environmental Health and Safety Department
**Effective date:** 2024-02-20
**Review cycle:** 36 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs GHS (Globally Harmonized System) labeling of chemical containers and management of the site Safety Data Sheet (SDS) library.

## Section 2. Primary Container Labeling

Clause 2.1 — Manufacturer-supplied containers shall retain their original GHS label (product identifier, signal word, hazard pictograms, hazard/precautionary statements) for the container's entire life; a damaged or removed label shall be replaced with an identical reprint before further use, not a generic substitute.

## Section 3. Workplace/Secondary Labeling

Clause 3.1 — A secondary container filled from bulk for immediate use by the person filling it, within the same shift, requires only the product identifier.

Clause 3.2 — A secondary container that will be used beyond the immediate shift, or by someone other than the person who filled it, requires full workplace labeling: product identifier, hazard information sufficient to convey the primary hazards, and the responsible department.

## Section 4. SDS Library

Clause 4.1 — A current SDS for every chemical in facility inventory shall be accessible at the point of use within the same shift it could be needed — electronic access counts if the system is available during a power outage or network failure, otherwise a physical binder is required at that location.

Clause 4.2 — A new chemical shall not be introduced to a work area until its SDS is added to the library and reviewed for any required engineering control, PPE, or storage segregation change.

## Section 5. Records

Clause 5.1 — SDS library contents are reviewed against actual chemical inventory annually; superseded SDS versions are retained for 30 years per exposure recordkeeping requirements.
