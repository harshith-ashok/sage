# SOP-DC-4000: Data Center and Control Room Equipment Safety

**Document owner:** IT Infrastructure Department
**Effective date:** 2024-06-15
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs physical safety and access controls for server rooms, data centers, and control room equipment racks supporting site operations.

## Section 2. Access Control

Clause 2.1 — Physical access to a data center or server room is restricted to authorized personnel via badge/biometric control; a visitor or contractor requires escort at all times and is logged in the access register.

## Section 3. Fire Suppression Interface

Clause 3.1 — Rooms with clean-agent fire suppression (e.g., FM-200, Novec) shall have the suppression system's pre-discharge alarm and abort-switch procedure posted at each entrance; personnel shall evacuate immediately upon the pre-discharge alarm unless actively aborting a false discharge per posted procedure.

## Section 4. Electrical Safety

Clause 4.1 — Rack-mounted equipment work (installation, cabling) on live power distribution units shall follow SOP-ELEC-500 for any exposed energized terminals; hot-swappable component replacement per manufacturer procedure does not require a full energized work permit.

## Section 5. Environmental Monitoring

Clause 5.1 — Temperature and humidity are continuously monitored with alarms at thresholds defined by the equipment manufacturer's operating specification; a sustained excursion beyond 30 minutes is escalated to IT Infrastructure Department management.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Access logs and environmental monitoring data are filed to the IT Infrastructure File and retained for 2 years.
