# SOP-CRANE-700: Crane and Rigging Lifting Operations

**Document owner:** Mechanical Department
**Effective date:** 2024-04-01
**Review cycle:** 24 months

## Section 1. Purpose and Scope

Governs planning and execution of crane lifts, rigging equipment inspection, and the critical-lift classification and review process.

## Section 2. Lift Classification

Clause 2.1 — A lift is classified "critical" if it exceeds 75% of the crane's rated capacity at the working radius, involves a load over a occupied structure or live process equipment, or uses two or more cranes (tandem lift).

Clause 2.2 — A critical lift requires a written lift plan reviewed by a qualified rigging engineer before the lift proceeds; a routine (non-critical) lift requires only a pre-lift check by the operator and rigger.

## Section 3. Rigging Equipment Inspection

Clause 3.1 — Slings, shackles, and rigging hardware shall be visually inspected before each use and shall bear a current color-coded inspection tag no older than 12 months.

Clause 3.3 — Any sling showing cut strands, crushing, or a missing/illegible capacity tag shall be removed from service immediately, not merely flagged for the next inspection cycle.

## Section 4. Exclusion Zone

Clause 4.1 — A ground-level exclusion zone equal to the maximum boom radius plus 10 feet shall be barricaded before the lift begins; personnel are prohibited from standing under a suspended load under any circumstance.

## Section 5. Weather Limits

Clause 5.1 — Lifting operations shall stop when sustained wind speed exceeds the crane manufacturer's rated limit for the load and configuration in use, or 30 mph, whichever is lower.

## Section 6. Records

Clause 6.1 — Critical lift plans and rigging inspection tags are filed to the Mechanical Lifting File and retained for 3 years.
